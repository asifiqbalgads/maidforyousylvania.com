03 What a deep clean covers

The list is the
whole point.

Anyone can say "deep clean." Here's the work behind it. Choose a room to see the kind of detail our teams are trained to look for — your own quote lists exactly what's included in your home.

  • Kitchen & appliances — degreased fronts, microwave, sink, bin
  • Bathrooms — tile, tub, toilets, grout, streak-free fixtures
  • Living & bedrooms — top-down dusting, glass, floors edge to edge
  • Details & edges — baseboards, vents, switches, door frames

Kitchen

  • Counters, backsplash and sink basin scrubbed and polished
  • Appliance exteriors degreased — range, fridge, dishwasher
  • Microwave inside and out
  • Cabinet fronts wiped; handles and pulls polished
  • Floors swept, mopped and edges reached
  • Trash removed and bin wiped

Bathrooms

  • Showers, tubs and tile scrubbed; soap scum lifted
  • Toilets fully cleaned, including base and behind
  • Vanities, mirrors and fixtures polished streak-free
  • Grout lines and caulk edges detailed
  • Exhaust vents and light switches wiped
  • Fresh linens hung when left out; floors mopped

Living & bedrooms

  • Dusting from the top down — ceilings fans, sills, shelves
  • Furniture surfaces, frames and decor wiped
  • Beds made to your preference; linens changed on request
  • Under-furniture vacuuming where accessible
  • Mirrors and glass polished
  • Carpets vacuumed edge to edge; hard floors mopped

Details & edges

  • Baseboards, door frames and door handles wiped
  • Light switches, outlets and thermostat plates cleaned
  • Vents, returns and ceiling corners dusted
  • Stair rails, banisters and spindles wiped
  • Interior glass, mirrors and patio door tracks
  • Spot-checked by a quality-control manager on rotation
